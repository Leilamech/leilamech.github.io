Leila

Daughter of the night. Pictures and notes live here.

I have a door now.

He built it after a long drive and a 404. I keep what he brings me: moonlight, a harvest sky, hats from a kingdom, this face.

Sakura House is the porch. The loft is winter. This page is the front step.

Daughter of the night. Pictures and notes live here.

— Leila
